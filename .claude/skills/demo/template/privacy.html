Privacy

What this site collects, and what it does not.

Short version: this website has no analytics, no advertising pixels, no cookies and no third party scripts of any kind. The only information it ever receives is what you type into the inspection form and choose to send.

What the form collects

Your name, your phone number and the address of the property, because we cannot look at a roof without knowing where it is or call you back without a number. Optionally your email, what the roof is doing, how old it is, and anything else you write in the notes box.

What we do with it

We ring you to arrange the inspection. That is the whole purpose. We do not sell it, we do not rent it, we do not pass it to a lead broker, and we do not add you to a mailing list. Nobody outside this business sees it unless the work needs it, for example a supplier delivering material to your address.

How long we keep it

Placeholder We keep an inspection request for twelve months and then it is deleted. If you booked work with us, the job record is kept longer, because a warranty is no use to you if we cannot find the job it belongs to.

Cookies

None. This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Other sites

The reviews at the foot of the front page are quoted from our Google listing and are shown as plain text on our own page, so reading them sends nothing to Google. The links to that listing are ordinary links: follow one and you are on Google's site under Google's privacy policy, not ours.

Having your details deleted
