What a replacement actually looks like.

The part most homeowners are nervous about is not the roof. It is having eight strangers, a dumpster and a day of noise in their driveway. Here is the whole thing, start to finish.

The look is free

Someone gets on the roof, not just in the driveway. You get told what is wrong, what is not, and whether it needs doing now or in three years.

A written price, and a date

What it costs, what you get for it, and the day the crew arrives. Customers keep mentioning the same thing in their reviews: they were told a date and the date held.

An early start

Crews are on site at six in the morning, because a Texas roof in the afternoon is a hard place to work and a slow one. Most houses are stripped, dried in and finished the same day.

The mess leaves with the crew

Magnet over the whole yard for nails, every scrap of old shingle in the dumpster, and the dumpster gone. Three separate customers have written that the place was left as if nothing had happened. That is the standard.

After a storm

A hail claim is mostly paperwork.

Hail does not always break a roof. It bruises the shingle, the granules come off, and the roof fails four years early. From the ground it looks like nothing happened, which is why so many of these claims never get filed.

  • Photographed from the roof

    Test squares, hail hits counted, the mat and the flashing. You get the pictures whatever you decide.

  • Your adjuster is not left alone up there

    So the damage is walked by two people who both know what they are looking at, instead of one person on a schedule.

  • Sometimes there is no claim

    Plenty of roofs after a storm are fine. Hearing that from a roofer costs you nothing and saves you a claim on your record.

What does a new roof cost?

It depends on the size of the roof, how many layers are coming off and what is under them. Anyone who quotes over the phone without seeing it is guessing. The look is free, then you get a written price that does not move unless there is rotten decking underneath, and you are shown that before it is replaced.

Most houses in __CITY_SHORT__ are one day. Crews start around six in the morning and are usually finished and cleaned up by late afternoon. Bigger or steeper roofs run into a second day, and we will tell you which one yours is before anything starts.
